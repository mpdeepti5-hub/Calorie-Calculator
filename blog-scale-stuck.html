What to do when the scale
feels stuck

Before you change everything, pause. Your body may be making progress in ways the scale cannot show yet.

03↗

A flat scale is information, not a verdict on your effort.

Body weight naturally moves up and down with hydration, digestion, hormones, stress, sleep, and salt. A few days — or even a couple of weeks — do not tell the whole story.

Look at the wider picture

Ask yourself: How are your clothes feeling? Is your energy steadier? Are you showing up for meals and movement more consistently? These changes matter, even when a single number is quiet.

If you weigh yourself, try looking at a weekly average rather than reacting to one reading. It gives you a calmer, more useful view of the trend.

Check before you change

Review the basics with curiosity. Have portions drifted because your hunger is higher? Has sleep been short? Have you had less everyday movement? There is no blame in any of these answers — they simply help you choose the next kind adjustment.

Keep your plan steady long enough to learn from it. Sustainable progress is allowed to be slow, uneven, and still successful.

How to build a balanced plate you’ll actually enjoy

A flexible formula for meals that feel satisfying, colourful, and completely yours.

02◒

A balanced plate is a helpful starting point, not another set of food rules.

Think of it as a way to bring a few different things together: energy, satisfaction, colour, and flavour. The exact foods can change with your culture, budget, appetite, and what is already in the kitchen.

The simple four-part formula

Start with a generous serving of vegetables or fruit for colour and fibre. Add a protein you enjoy, such as eggs, beans, yoghurt, fish, tofu, or chicken. Choose a carbohydrate for energy — rice, potatoes, roti, oats, pasta, or bread. Then finish with a little fat or flavour, like nuts, seeds, olive oil, avocado, or your favourite chutney.

You do not need every part to look perfect at every meal. The pattern matters more than a single plate.

Let satisfaction count

A meal that looks “healthy” but leaves you thinking about snacks an hour later is not doing its whole job. Include foods you genuinely like and eat slowly enough to notice when you feel comfortably full.

When meals are satisfying, you are less likely to feel caught in the restrict-and-rebound cycle. Nourishment can be both practical and pleasurable.
